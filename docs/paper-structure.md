# Paper Structure — Federated Learning Report (PYT / FedLearning)

**Total length:** ~3,800–4,000 words

---

## 1. Abstract (~250 words)
- Motivation
- Objective
- Methods
- Results
- Novelty
- **6 keywords**

## 2. Introduction (500–600 words)
- 4–5 citations
- 1. Objective
- 2. Contribution, motivation & problem statement

## 3. Literature Review (300–400 words)
- 10–12 citations

## 4. Research Gap (100–150 words)

## 5. Proposed Methodology (~1,000 words)
- 5–6 diagrams
- Includes:
  1. Dataset / data preparation
  2. Feature engineering
  3. Federated model parameters (with equations)
- Process description

## 6. Results & Discussion (~150 of 2,000 words section budget, incl. data)
1. Results + diagrams (short)
2. Justification (tables, graphs)
- Discussion: critically assess use cases and **implications**
- Observations & findings
  - → Validation
  - → Analysis

## 7. Conclusion & Future Scope

## 8. References
- **15–17 references** total

---

Resource allocation & planning
